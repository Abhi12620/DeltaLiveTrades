Notification settings

Alerts fire from your browser while this tab is open and the dashboard is actively polling. On mobile Chrome, keep the tab active (screen on) for checks to run — background/locked tabs are throttled by the OS, so neither browser notifications nor Telegram messages will fire while fully backgrounded. Telegram delivers as a real push once the message is sent, which is more noticeable than an in-page notification.

Create a bot via @BotFather, then message your bot once and fetch https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates to find your chat id.
